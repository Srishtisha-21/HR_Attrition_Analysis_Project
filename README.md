# HR_Attrition_Analysis_Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset.

The goal of this project is to identify the major factors associated with employee turnover, including department, job role, overtime, salary, job satisfaction, age group, business travel, and years at the company.

The analysis was performed using PostgreSQL, with Power BI used to create an interactive dashboard for business reporting and visualization.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect team performance.

The objective of this analysis is to answer questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which job roles experience the most employee turnover?
- Does overtime relate to higher attrition?
- Does salary level affect employee retention?
- Is low job satisfaction associated with higher attrition?
- Which age groups have the highest attrition?
- Does employee tenure affect attrition?
- Which employee groups may require additional retention attention?
